Mercedes-Benz

Mercedes-Benz

Al Mulla Automobiles Co. is the sole authorized General Distributor of Mercedes-Benz in Kuwait since 2019. The partnership between Mercedes-Benz Group AG and Al Mulla Automobiles Co. is built on common core values and competencies that forged a new alliance with the vision of putting the customer first and offering the best customer experience. As an automotive pioneer, Mercedes-Benz is one of the most celebrated and admired marquees, amongst the luxury automobile brands in the world.


In 2020, Al Mulla Automobiles Co., received the First Place Award in the Mercedes-Benz Overseas General Distributor Awards for Sales and Customer Services. This was the first award of its kind to be presented to a distributor after only one year in operation.


Al Mulla Group continues to invest with the Mercedes-Benz brand through its strategic growth plans to build a comprehensive network of showrooms, service centers and facilities that caters to the brand’s image of luxury and exceptional driving experience.
